# v7.6 rulings (map and home headings fix, 2026-11-19)

A small release ahead of the "Food from the land" pack, which becomes v7.7. Jordan asked for both fixes
and said to ship them; the choices below were made while building and he can overrule any of them.

## What happened

- USGS retired the **USGS Imagery Topo** base map service after 2026-11-16 (notice dated 2026-10-13:
  https://www.usgs.gov/the-national-map-data-delivery/news/imagery-topo-base-map-retirement). The server
  now answers "Service USGSImageryTopo/MapServer not found". The app's "USGS Imagery + Topo" layer drew a
  blank map and said nothing. USGS named no replacement.
- On the Survival home every section heading sat flush against the card above it. Cause: each heading is
  the first child of its own section, and a `:first-child` rule removed its top margin.

## Rulings

1. **Replacement layer is USGS Imagery Only** (`USGSImageryOnly`, id `sat`, shown as "USGS Imagery").
   Satellite photos with no contours, roads or names. It is the only imagery service left on the USGS
   base map server (the others are USGSTopo, USGSHydroCached, USGSShadedReliefOnly). Cost if wrong: none
   beyond the lost contours; USGS Topo is unchanged.
2. **The retired layer is kept, not deleted** (id `imagery`, shown as "USGS Imagery + Topo (retired)").
   It is listed in Layers only while an area saved from it exists, and it can't be saved again. Deleting
   the id would have dropped those saved areas from the list and left their tiles on the phone with no way
   to remove them.
3. **A phone left on the retired layer with nothing saved from it opens on USGS Imagery**, not USGS Topo:
   the person chose satellite.
4. **A notice appears on the map when a whole screen of tiles fails**, with a "Choose another map" button.
   Three wordings: the retired layer; offline outside saved areas; online but not loading. A tile that
   falls back to a saved lower zoom counts as loaded, so the notice does not appear inside a saved area.
5. **Size estimate for USGS Imagery uses the old layer's 45 KB per tile.** Not measured: tiles could not
   be fetched from the build session. The save dialog's estimate may be off for this layer.
6. **Home section headings** (Pinned, Recent, Topics, Tools, Checklists on Survival; Pinned and Recent on
   Emergency) are 14 px muted capitals with 32 px of space above each Survival section. Category rows
   stay 17 px. Jordan had asked to see before-and-after pictures first, then said to ship; pictures were
   sent with the release.
7. **Not verified from the build session:** an actual USGS Imagery tile for Central New York (the session
   cannot fetch tiles; the service description and its tile levels were read). Jordan's phone check covers it.
