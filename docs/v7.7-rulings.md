**Made while building:**

   round: a first try that did so put Asthma attack and Chest pain above CPR for "breathing" (their keyword is "short of breath").
   Cost: "severe" now lists Amputation ("severed") first, ahead of Anaphylaxis and Severe bleeding.

   layered lists. They are replaced by one list of the first five on screen for the same 123 searches as v7.7 shows them

   words put their protocol on the first screen. `docs/v7.7-search-comparison.md` shows every list beside v7.6.

## Keeping meat: methods

| Method | Label | Source | End step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fridge and freezer (times table) | Backed by a food-safety agency | Minnesota Department of Health; FoodSafety.gov; shorter times from Utah State and Clemson extension | Cook to 165°F (the page's general rule) |
| Hanging a carcass in the cold | Backed by a food-safety agency (NYS DOH 45°F limit); times from Penn State, Cornell, NDSU, NMSU extension | NYS DOH; Penn State used | Butcher, then keep or cook; cook to 165°F |
| Winter cold as the freezer | Traditional: historical source | US Army FM 21-76 (2006 copy) | Cook to 165°F before eating |
| Jerky (dehydrator or oven at 140°F) | Backed by a food-safety agency | NCHFP jerky page and review; USDA; UAF for game and bear | Deer jerky pre-heated to 170°F or more: eat as it is within two weeks at room temperature (NCHFP); any other jerky: cook to 165°F |
| Smoked deer jerky (brine, cooler, smoke 150°F, air dry) | Traditional: extension source | NDSU FN155 | Cook to 165°F before eating |
| Smoking over a smoke fire | Traditional: historical source | US Army FM 21-76 (2006 copy) | Cook to 165°F before eating (the manual says no cooking needed; not followed) |
| Sun and air drying | Traditional: historical source | US Army FM 21-76 (2006 copy) | Cook to 165°F before eating |
| Salting or brining (saltwater soak) | Traditional: historical source | US Army FM 21-76 (2006 copy) | Wash off salt; cook to 165°F before eating |
| Corning game (corned venison) | Traditional: extension source | NDSU FN155; commercial cure mix only (NCHFP) | Simmer about five hours; 165°F in the thickest part |

Left out (no opened source, or not given): pemmican (no source opened); dry cure and brine cure of whole legs (NDSU; not given,
ruling 7); cured, dried or fermented sausage (spec); canning (no procedure; Preserving food); grinding without power; stock and rendered
fat.
