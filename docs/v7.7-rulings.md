### Plant poisoning gate

What the sources a household can open say to do when someone eats a poisonous plant (all opened 2026-11-19; URLs in the content review):

- **Poison Control (National Capital Poison Center).** Pokeberries: "Rinse the mouth and give water or milk to drink", then use
  webPOISONCONTROL or call 1-800-222-1222; watching a child at home is often enough. Poison hemlock: act immediately, go to an
  emergency department, call the poison center or use the online tool, do not treat symptoms at home.
- **Upstate New York Poison Center** (the regional center for Central New York; Poisonous Plants, 2024): "Remove any portion left in
  the mouth."; call the poison center; give a brief history; the specialist says what to do next. Cooking does not destroy plant
  poisons; do not assume any plant is non-poisonous.
- **Northern New England Poison Center** and **Vermont Department of Health** (false hellebore): do not wait for symptoms; contact the
  poison center at once; most people need hospital care.
- **CDC**: no CDC page found tells a household what to do after eating a plant. NIOSH's Poisonous Plants and Work (October 1, 2026) says
  only that many plants are poisonous "when ingested" and gives first aid for skin contact. CDC MMWR (2021) on mushrooms: never eat a
  wild mushroom unless an expert identified it.
- **NYS Department of Health** (Poison Proof Your Home, revised December 2025): stay calm, call Poison Control at once, have the
  person's condition, age and weight, the label and the time ready, and follow the center's instructions. Nothing plant-specific.
- **HRSA Poison Help** (Plants FAQ): the questions are listed without answers; call the Poison Help Line.

**Does `src/protocols/poisoning.json` cover it?** Mostly. It already says: do not make them vomit; keep the plant or a sample of vomit
"if it was a plant or mushroom"; do not give food or drink unless Poison Control advises; do not wait for symptoms; call Poison Control
if you have signal; with no signal, evacuate anyone who swallowed "a wild mushroom or plant". **Gaps (PASS: logged, protocol not
edited):** removing plant left in the mouth (Upstate New York Poison Center) and rinsing the mouth (Poison Control) are not in it; Poison
Control's "give water or milk" after pokeberries differs from the protocol's "nothing unless Poison Control tells you to"; no source
gives steps for a household with no Poison Control line and no hospital. Logged as D156, marked "Medical professional". Wild plants links
`p:poisoning` at the moment of decision (first block, and its own closing section) and says only "don't wait for symptoms".
