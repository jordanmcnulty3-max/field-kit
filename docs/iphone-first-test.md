# Field Kit: first iPhone test

Thanks for trying this. It has never been tested on an iPhone,
so anything odd you find helps.

## 1. Get the app

1. Use Safari, not Chrome.
2. Open https://jordanmcnulty3-max.github.io/field-kit/
3. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
4. Open it from the new Home Screen icon.

## 2. Tell me first

- Your iPhone model.
- Your iOS version (Settings, General, About).
- Did you open it from the Home Screen icon or in Safari?

## 3. Checklist

Do each step and look for the thing named.
Screenshot it if anything looks wrong.

1. Open the app. Three tabs at the bottom? Nothing hidden under the notch or the home bar?
2. Tap Tactical, then Map. Does the map draw? Do pinch-zoom and drag work? Does the grid reading by the crosshair change as you drag?
3. Location: Safari asks to use your location. Allow it. Does a blue dot appear? Tap Position at the top of Tactical. Does it show a grid?
4. Compass: Tap Position at the top of Tactical and scroll down to the compass. Hold the phone flat, the tall way (not turned sideways). If a Turn on compass button appears, tap it and choose Allow. If no button appears, just turn the phone and watch the heading number. Does the number change as you turn? Does north on the screen match a real compass or the Maps app? If it says the compass is blocked, tell Jordan exactly what you tapped and screenshot it. If it says the compass may need calibrating and that does not go away after moving the phone in a figure-8, screenshot it.
5. Mark: on the Map, press Mark, type a name, pick a kind, tap Save. Does the marker show on the map?
6. Open a sheet (press Mark again), then use the back swipe. Does the sheet close without leaving the app?
7. Tap the Tools button (the ruler icon on the right edge of the map), then Measure. Drag the map so the crosshair is on a first point and press Add point. Drag to a second point and press Add point. Does the reading make sense? Then press Done.
8. Go to a marker (a location fix and the compass must be on): tap your marker on the map, then Go to. On Position, does the arrow point at it as you turn?
9. Keep screen on (on Position): does the screen stay awake?
10. Save map for offline (in Tools): frame a small area and download it. Turn on Airplane Mode. Does the map still show that area?
11. Close the app fully (swipe it away). Reopen it in Airplane Mode. Does it open? Are the marker and the saved map still there?
12. Tools, Export, Save file: where does the file go? Can you share it?
13. Tools, Import: does the file picker open?
14. Tap the moon button at the top right (red light mode). Is everything readable?
15. Type a number in Ballistics or in the declination box on Position (tap By hand). Does the keyboard have a minus sign and a decimal point?
16. If you can, leave the app unused for a week. Are the marker and the saved map still there?

## 4. Send results

For each problem, send:

- the step number,
- what happened,
- a screenshot.

After step 4: did the Turn on compass button appear at all?
Does it ask again each time you reopen the app?

Steps that worked: just say "all fine except ...".
