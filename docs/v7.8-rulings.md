# v7.8 rulings (Tactical map)

## Dropped

- Track climb figure: dropped. Phone GPS altitude noise made every method tried read a phantom climb on flat ground (about 76 m at 5 m noise) and overstate real climbs by a third or more. Needs barometer data or a terrain model; not attempted in v7.8.

- QR sharing between phones: dropped from v7.8 by the owner; plans move between phones as GPX or KML files. Plan Task 11 (QR) was replaced by "iPhone first-test fixes" (compass permission, a timeout fallback for older iOS, a tester's checklist).

## Accepted limits

- Time to go is an estimate. With a poor GPS signal, during the first minute of walking it can read up to 2.5 times the true time for a few seconds (the tests allow 2.5 times; the worst measured was 2.18 to 2.31 times). It never shows while standing still, and it clears within 60 seconds of stopping (the tests allow 60 s; measured 45 to 48 s). It needs about 30 seconds of walking toward the target before it appears.
- A marker or ring sheet with unsaved changes asks before Escape closes it, but if Escape is pressed again with no tap in between, the browser may not let the app stop it, and the sheet closes without asking.

The Turn on compass button and the 4-second "no sensor" message after permission follow the brief;
the pages above did not change the code, except that the blocked message names no setting (see the row above).

Chrome 152 and later (desktop and Android) also has `DeviceOrientationEvent.requestPermission`, so having the function does not
mean iPhone. The compass therefore listens for readings on every phone from the start. On a browser with the function it shows
Turn on compass only if no reading has come 800 ms after the compass starts, and keeps it until permission is given or a reading
arrives; the 4-second "no sensor" message waits for the permission. On Android Chrome the first reading is expected to come
sooner, so the button is expected never to appear (not seen on a device). Where Chrome's motion sensor setting is Block, the button appears and tapping it shows the blocked
message, whose wording fits any phone.

`AbortSignal.timeout` is called only through `timeoutSignal(ms)` (src/map.js and src/sw.js), which falls back to an
`AbortController` and a timer, and to no time limit with no `AbortController`. Before this, on iOS below 16, the map server
check failed every time (Save map for offline said the server could not be reached) and the service worker always served the
cached page, so updates never arrived.

Where `navigator.share` is missing, or fails for any reason other than the user closing the share sheet, Share position copies
the text and shows it in a sheet, so the feature is safe either way.

## Decisions made during the build (each with its cost if wrong)

Behaviour a person will notice:

- A failed save (storage full) keeps the item on screen for the session and shows a message every time. Cost: the item is gone after a reload if the message is missed.
- "Go to" on a marker saves pending edits first. Cost: Go to on a new marker creates it.
- Areas and rings are selected by their line or name label, never their fill. Cost: an area cannot be opened by tapping its middle.
- Back, Escape or a backdrop tap on a marker or ring with unsaved changes asks "Keep editing" or "Discard". The sheet's own Cancel button discards without asking.
- An unfinished route or area left by a tab tap or Search is kept in memory and comes back when the Map shows again. Cost: a page reload loses it.
- New markers always start as Point, not the last kind used. Cost: one more tap when marking several of one kind.
- A fix older than 30 seconds is "Last position, ... ago" everywhere, with no speed. Cost: the label may flicker under trees; one constant.
- A track records a point only beyond max(5 m, twice the fix accuracy), and a leg counts as moving at 0.3 m/s or more with a gap up to 10 minutes. Cost: tracks are coarse in poor accuracy (corners cut on a winding trail), and a walk across a gap of up to 10 minutes with no fixes counts as moving. Simulated only; check on a phone.
- Starting a recording over a track last touched more than 30 minutes ago asks "Continue the old track" or "Start a new track". Cost: one question after a long stop.
- Time to go uses closing speed toward the target (30 s and 6 fixes minimum, scatter threshold, one bit of hysteresis). Cost: no time while walking across or around the target; long on winding paths; see Accepted limits.
- Arrival does not latch: walking back out past the arrival distance clears "Arrived".
- A sighting (friend, hostile, neutral, unknown) older than 24 hours is drawn dashed and unfilled.
- KML altitude is kept only when the file says it is absolute or the file is Field Kit's own. Cost: altitudes from other apps' ground-clamped KML are dropped.
- Import treats two unnamed items of the same kind at the same place (within about 1.1 m) as one.
- Distance rounding moves a value into the next band (999.6 m shows 1.00 km).
- The compass heading is shown with a "may need calibrating" hint when an iPhone flags it, not hidden. Cost: a reading the phone marks invalid is on screen with a warning.
- The "Allow compass" button appears only after 0.8 s with no reading; the blocked message names no iPhone setting (none could be verified).
- Emergency: on a question step the question now sits in the fixed bar directly above Yes and No, on every protocol. Layout only; no protocol text, order or data changed. Reason: in "CPR, adult" step 1 the question the buttons answer was off-screen and opposite in sense to the heading. Cost: every question step looks different from v7.7; a long question takes reading space. The heading wording is unchanged and still asks the opposite-sense question: a content decision for the owner.

Checks and sources:

- Geodesic distances are checked against one method (Vincenty: a worked pair and GeographicLib cases), not two. The 50 km ring bound is argued from that check on a sphere, not measured against an ellipsoid.
- The WMM2025 coefficient table could not be opened by a reviewer; it is confirmed through the 12 NOAA test values and field vectors to 0.05 nT. The year is clamped to the model's window.
- `FK._test` (fix and heading hooks for tests) ships in the build.
- A small CSS change to `.mk-act` was kept as a tidy with no test behind it.

Left as found (not in this release): the search order for "tourniquet" (search results are pinned); shorthand grids in Go to grid; starting a route at the nearest point instead of point 1; the Tools icon; list speed at 2,000 markers; two browser tabs writing at once (last one wins, as in v7.7); the area of a self-crossing outline; the Daylight hint that names Syracuse's latitude.
