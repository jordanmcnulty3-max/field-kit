# v7.7 rulings (Food from the land pack)

Decisions made during the v7.7 build. Jordan's own decisions are marked; the rest were made while building and he can overrule them.

## Search (done first, before any content)

**Jordan's decisions, 2026-11-19:**

1. **Results are one list in rank order, with no section headings** (he chose this over "Emergency always on top" and "top three,
   then groups"). Each row still names its kind. Before, the overlay regrouped results by kind and showed the leading kind's whole
   group first, so a protocol ranked second could be nineteenth on screen.
2. **A searched word that is a whole keyword outranks a title that merely starts with it.** Before, "fall" showed three fallout pages
   ahead of Head injury.

**Made while building:**

3. **The whole-keyword score is 5.25**: above a title prefix (5), below a whole title word (5.5). Common little words ("in", "of",
   "no" and the like) get nothing, as before. This replaces the v7.3 rule that applied only to one- and two-letter searches.
4. **A word with an ending counts as the whole word, one way only.** Searching "collapse" treats the keyword "collapsed" as whole;
   "vomit" finds "vomiting"; "burn" finds "Burns". Endings: s, es, d, ed, ing. Without it, a first try of ruling 2 put Shock and two
   house pages above CPR for "collapse", and Clean and disinfect above Severe vomiting for "vomit". It is not applied the other way
   round: a first try that did so put Asthma attack and Chest pain above CPR for "breathing" (their keyword is "short of breath").
   Cost: "severe" now lists Amputation ("severed") first, ahead of Anaphylaxis and Severe bleeding.
5. **The old pinned lists were replaced, not patched.** The tests pinned the first three on screen against v7.3 and v7.4 in several
   layered lists. They are replaced by one list of the first five on screen for the same 123 searches as v7.7 shows them
   (`tests/fixtures/search_v77_on_screen.json`), a test that the screen order is the rank order, and a test that fourteen hurried
   words put their protocol on the first screen. `docs/v7.7-search-comparison.md` shows every list beside v7.6.

**Measured (full record in the comparison doc):**

- Protocols whose own title or keyword does not put them in the first three: **26 in v7.5, 10 now**; none added. Fixed: "tourniquet"
  and "wound packing" (Severe bleeding), "food poisoning" (Vomiting and diarrhea), "dressing" (Wound care), "cold hands" and "cold
  feet" (Frostbite), "generator" (Carbon monoxide), "hot water", "boiling water" and "stove" (Burns), "river" (Drowning), "storm"
  (Lightning), "fuel" (Poisoning), "dirty bomb" (Radiation decontamination), "cold sweat" (Shock), "wet and cold" (Hypothermia).
- Still outside the first three: Severe bleeding for "wound"; Hypothermia for "cold" (6th, behind two pages titled with the word,
  Drowning and Frostbite) and "cold water" (4th); Trauma check for "shot"; Radiation sickness for "nuclear fallout" (5th); Recovery
  position for "breathing" and "unresponsive" (behind the three CPR protocols); Shock for "collapse" (behind CPR and Fainting); Wound
  care for "cut" and "bite".
- **Worse than v7.6: "light" no longer shows Lightning strike in the first five** (SOS light, Fainting, Daylight and moon lead; it
  appears from "lightn"). Before, a title starting with the word outranked the keyword "light headed".
- "baby food" still shows the babies page sixth; "gas stove" shows Cook without power fifth. Both are ranking questions the list
  change does not touch.
- Of 2,257 generated searches, the first three changed for 794 with the list change and for a further 166 with the scoring change.
  Most are three-letter fragments where weak matches used to ride in with their group.
